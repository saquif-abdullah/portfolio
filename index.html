Machine Vision / Calibration

Precision tile calibration & stitching

Designed calibration approaches for large-area inspection where hundreds of camera tiles must align accurately. Worked with circle-grid calibration, affine/rigid transforms, lens distortion, coordinate transformation and per-tile compensation.

  • Supported images up to 80,000 × 80,000 pixels.
  • Eliminated a stitched calibration map consuming 30+ GB by moving toward compact per-tile geometric information.
  • Investigating calibration strategies specifically for continuous “fly capture”, where motion and image timing change the error model.
